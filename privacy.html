Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What data is collected

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the tool receives from TikTok's Login Kit: the account's open ID, display name and avatar, plus an access token and refresh token needed to upload videos on the owner's behalf (scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

How it is used

This data is used only to authenticate the owner and to upload the owner's scheduled videos to the owner's own TikTok account. Nothing is shared with third parties and nothing is used for advertising or analytics.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored on the owner's private server and never published or transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's official API endpoints.

Withdrawing consent

The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok: Settings > Security > Manage apps, or by deleting the connected data from the server.
